# [FICTIONAL COMPANY] Refunds, Returns, & Exchanges Policy

**Disclaimer:** This is a fictional document created for demonstration and testing of the AI Customer Support RAG Agent. It does not represent any real organization.

---

## 1. 30-Day Return Window

- Customers may return any eligible product within **30 calendar days** from the date the order was delivered.
- To qualify for a full refund:
  - The item must be in new, unused condition with all original packaging, manual inserts, and included cables/accessories intact.
  - Proof of purchase (such as an order confirmation email or invoice number) is required.
  - Return requests initiated after 30 days are ineligible for refunds but may be considered for store credit up to 45 days upon manager approval.

---

## 2. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are strictly non-refundable and non-returnable:
- Digital gift cards, downloadable software licenses, and extended digital service plans.
- Consumable cleaning kits and sanitary ear tips that have been opened or unsealed.
- Custom engraved or personalized hardware.
- Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance As-Is" at checkout.

---

## 3. Return Shipping Fees & Restocking

- **Defective or Incorrect Items**: We provide a pre-paid printable return shipping label at zero cost to you.
- **Buyer's Remorse or Change of Mind**: A flat return shipping and handling deduction of **$6.50** will be withheld from your final refund amount.
- **Restocking Fees**: There are **no restocking fees** for items returned in unopened original condition. Open-box returns with missing non-essential plastic packaging are subject to a 10% inspection fee.

---

## 4. Refund Processing & Timelines

1. **Inspection**: Once our warehouse receives the returned package, our quality assurance team completes physical inspection within **2 to 3 business days**.
2. **Payment Reversal**: Once approved, the refund is initiated directly to your original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, or PayPal).
3. **Bank Posting Time**:
   - Credit card providers: 3 to 7 business days to reflect on your statement.
   - PayPal: Within 24 hours of release.
   - Store Credit: Immediately applied to your account.

---

## 5. How to Initiate a Return

1. Visit our Returns Portal at `https://returns.fictional-brand.example`.
2. Enter your 8-digit order number (e.g., `#ORD-92811`) and billing zip code.
3. Select the items you wish to return and choose your return reason.
4. Download and print the generated prepaid shipping label.
5. Affix the label to the exterior of the box and drop it off at any authorized shipping drop location.
