# [FICTIONAL COMPANY] Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Domestic Shipping Options & Transit Times

We offer three primary shipping tiers across the continental United States:

1. **Standard Ground Shipping**:
   - Delivery window: 3 to 5 business days.
   - Cost: Free on all orders totaling $50 or more; otherwise a flat rate of $4.99 applies.
   - Carriers: USPS, UPS Ground, and FedEx Home Delivery.

2. **Expedited 2-Day Air**:
   - Delivery window: 2 business days (orders placed before 1:00 PM EST ship the same day).
   - Cost: Flat rate of $12.99 regardless of order value.
   - Carriers: UPS 2nd Day Air and FedEx 2Day.

3. **Overnight Express**:
   - Delivery window: Next business day afternoon delivery.
   - Cost: $24.99 flat rate.
   - Weekend delivery: Saturday delivery is available in select metropolitan zip codes for an additional $10.00 surcharge.

---

## 2. Order Processing & Cut-off Times

- Orders submitted Monday through Friday before **1:00 PM EST** are processed and dispatched on the same business day.
- Orders submitted after 1:00 PM EST, on weekends, or during federal holidays will be processed on the following business day.
- You will receive an automated shipping confirmation email containing your tracking number within 2 hours of package dispatch.

---

## 3. International Shipping

- We ship to over 45 international destinations including Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, and Japan.
- **International Standard**: 7 to 14 business days ($18.00 - $28.00 depending on weight).
- **International Express**: 3 to 5 business days ($39.00 flat rate).
- **Customs, Duties & Taxes**: For Canada and the European Union, import taxes and VAT are collected at checkout (DDP - Delivery Duty Paid). For all other countries, customs fees must be paid by the recipient upon parcel arrival.

---

## 4. Lost, Damaged, or Delayed Packages

- **Delayed Shipments**: If your tracking has not updated for more than 4 business days, contact our support team at `support@fictional-brand.example` to initiate a carrier trace.
- **Damaged Packages**: Please take photos of the damaged shipping box and damaged items, and submit a claim within 48 hours of receipt. Replacement units are dispatched via expedited shipping at no cost.
- **Address Changes**: Delivery addresses cannot be changed once the order enters the 'Fulfillment' status. Customers must contact the carrier directly to request a package intercept.
