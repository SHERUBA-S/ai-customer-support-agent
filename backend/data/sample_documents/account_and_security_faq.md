## 1. Password Reset & Account Recovery

- **Forgot Password**: Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen and enter your registered email address. A password reset link valid for **30 minutes** will be delivered to your inbox.
- **Did Not Receive Email**:
  - Check your spam, promotions, or junk folders.
  - Verify you entered the exact email address used at registration.
  - If third-party sign-in (Google / Apple ID) was originally used, sign in using that provider instead of password entry.
- **Account Lockout**: After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, your account is temporarily locked for **15 minutes** for protection against brute-force attacks.

---

## 2. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

- We strongly recommend enabling Two-Factor Authentication under **Settings > Security**.
- Supported 2FA methods:
  - Authenticator Apps (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password).
  - SMS Text Verification (available in supported countries).
- **Lost 2FA Device**: When you configure 2FA, you are provided with **10 emergency backup codes**. If you lose your phone, enter any one of these one-time codes on the 2FA prompt.

## 3. Subscription Plans & Billing Cycles

We offer two subscription tiers for cloud audio backup and premium equalizer presets:

1. **Standard Plan**:
   - $4.99 per month, or $49.00 billed annually (save 18%).
   - Includes 50 GB cloud audio storage and 3 connected devices.

2. **Pro Family Plan**:
   - $9.99 per month, or $99.00 billed annually.
   - Includes unlimited cloud presets, up to 10 family devices, and priority support dispatch.

- **Cancellation**: You can cancel your subscription at any time under **Account > Billing > Manage Subscription**. You will retain access until the end of your current billing period. No prorated partial-month cash refunds are issued for cancellations mid-cycle.

## 4. Deleting Your Account & Data Privacy (GDPR / CCPA)

- To permanently delete your account, navigate to **Settings > Privacy > Delete Account**.
- Once confirmed via email, all personal data, saved presets, and transaction logs will be permanently scrubbed from our active production database within **14 calendar days**.
- In accordance with financial record-keeping laws, invoice records are archived in cold storage for 7 years.
