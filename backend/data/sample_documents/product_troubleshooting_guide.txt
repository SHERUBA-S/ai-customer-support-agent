SECTION 1: DEVICE WILL NOT POWER ON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If your smart hub or audio device is unresponsive:
Step 1: Check power connection. Verify the USB-C power cable is firmly seated into
both the device port and the wall adapter. Do not use non-certified unbranded chargers.
Step 2: Perform a Hard Reset:
  - Disconnect the USB-C cable from the back of the device.
  - Press and hold the primary POWER button for 15 seconds while disconnected.
  - While still holding the button, reconnect the USB-C cable.
  - Release the button after the LED indicator pulses blue twice.
Step 3: Power Outlet Check. Plug the adapter into a verified working wall outlet
(test with a lamp or phone charger).
Step 4: If the LED indicator remains dark after 30 minutes of charging, the internal
power capacitor may have experienced a surge. Contact warranty support for a replacement.
